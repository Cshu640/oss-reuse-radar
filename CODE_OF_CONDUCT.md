# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

We want a practical, respectful project where people can question assumptions, report defects, and improve the product without personal attacks. Be clear, patient, and considerate. Discuss evidence and trade-offs rather than the person.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, deliberate exposure of private data or secrets, and knowingly misleading claims about tests, security, adoption, or project readiness.

## Enforcement

The project is not yet public and has no configured enforcement team. For now, maintainers will document concerns locally and may remove or decline contributions that violate this standard. A public contact and escalation process must be added before public launch.
